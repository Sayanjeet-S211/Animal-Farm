Who's whoThe story behind the story

Orwell wrote Animal Farm as a fable about the Russian Revolution (1917) and Stalin's rule. Each animal stands for a real person or group.

Power is rarely grabbed in one day. It grows through small steps, rewritten rules and silence, unless people ask questions and remember.
