Animal Farm · George Orwell · 1945Group poster · A Warning in Seven Commandments
Our group's reading

Who holds the paintbrush?

Animal Farm is usually read as the Russian Revolution in disguise. We think it is also about something simpler: the pigs win by controlling what is written down, so the other animals end up doubting their own memories.

How it happens, in four steps →
1The promise · Ch. II

Seven rules on the barn wall

1. Whatever goes upon two legs is an enemy.
2. Whatever goes upon four legs, or has wings, is a friend.
3. No animal shall wear clothes.
4. No animal shall sleep in a bed with sheets.
5. No animal shall drink alcohol to excess.
6. No animal shall kill any other animal without cause.
7. All animals are equal.
By Ch. X only one rule is left: ALL ANIMALS ARE EQUAL BUT SOME ANIMALS ARE MORE EQUAL THAN OTHERS
Red words = added later by the pigs, each time they broke a rule.
In simple termsThe rules the animals fought for were quietly rewritten to suit the pigs.
2The weakness

Most animals can't read well

Pigsread and write perfectly
Benjaminreads well, but says nothing
Murielreads a little
Cloverknows letters, not words
Boxernever gets past D
Sheeponly learn a slogan
In simple termsIf you can't read the rules, you can't tell when someone changes them.
3The trick · Ch. VI

"Is it written down?"

“Is it written down anywhere?”SQUEALER, WHEN THE ANIMALS REMEMBER A RULE

The animals remember a rule against trading with humans. It was never written down, so Squealer convinces them they dreamed it. When something is written down, the pigs simply repaint it.

In simple termsNo record means "it never happened". A changed record means "you remembered it wrong".
4The result · Ch. X

Power grows step by step

1A noble cause
2Small perks
3Rival chased out
4Rules rewritten
5Pigs = humans

At the end the animals look from pig to man and can't tell them apart.

In simple termsNo single step looked like a takeover, so nobody stopped it.
Our message: whoever controls the record controls the past.Read it for the Commandments, not just the Revolution.
